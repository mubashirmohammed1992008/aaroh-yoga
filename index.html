Still wondering if Aaroh is for you? Drop us a note — we’re happy to help.

Do I need experience to join? +

No. Our programs are designed to welcome beginners and offer options so you can move at a pace that feels right.

How do the online programs work? +

After we confirm your order, you’ll receive access details by email. You can practise from home and revisit your materials whenever you like.

What do I need to get started? +

A comfortable space, a mat or soft floor, and a few minutes for yourself. Props are optional; a folded blanket or cushion can help.
